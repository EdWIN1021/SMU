## How To Play

- Press F1 to toggle Debug/Developer Mode.
- Press F8 to restart the game.
- Press Q to quit the game.
- Press O to step one frame.
- Press P to toggle Pause.
- Press I to spawn a new asteroid (maximum 12).
- Press N to respawn the Player Ship if it is dead.
- Press Space to fire bullets (maximum 20).

- Hold T to enable Slow Mode.
- Hold E to accelerate the Player Ship.
- Hold S to spin the Player Ship left.
- Hold F to spin the Player Ship right.
